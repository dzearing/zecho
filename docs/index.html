One-Key Capture

Hold the FN key to record, release to transcribe. Double-tap to lock recording. It stays out of your way until you need it.

Local AI Cleanup

Removes filler words, fixes corrections, and polishes grammar — all with a local model. Choose from Light, Medium, or High cleanup.

Clipboard History

Every transcription is saved. Click any past result to copy it again. Your voice notes are always a click away.

Instant Results

Whisper runs locally on your hardware. Transcription starts the moment you stop speaking — no waiting for cloud roundtrips.

Your Style

Formal, Casual, or Very Casual — pick how your text reads. Custom prompts let you fine-tune exactly how AI shapes your words.

Auto Updates

Zecho checks for updates automatically. New models, features, and fixes arrive seamlessly — no manual downloads.
